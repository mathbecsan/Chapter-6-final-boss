THE CONDUCTOR

The Infinity Train's last stop. Wear the Conductor's health bar down to zero — the notches mark his five phases, and he only gets angrier until he's fully enraged. You have 4 lives, no checkpoints, and 3 shields: tap X to raise one for 1.5 seconds and block everything. They fully recharge every time you break a phase, so spend them on the attacks you can't dodge.
